# Cybersecurity Journey — Mid-August to 1 October 2026

This page documents the first phase of my cybersecurity journey, from starting in mid-August 2026 through 1 October 2026.

## Starting point

I started cybersecurity in mid-August 2026 with limited technical knowledge and no established specialization.

I quickly began learning through practical TryHackMe rooms rather than following a purely theoretical path. This let me discover networking, Linux, web applications, reconnaissance, exploitation and defensive security through hands-on exercises.

Over this period, I moved from basic concepts toward being able to work more independently in authorized lab environments.

## Reconnaissance and enumeration

I learned to approach a target by first gathering information rather than immediately attempting exploitation.

Practical areas included:

- Host discovery

Relevant TryHackMe rooms include **Nmap Live Host Discovery**, **Nmap**, **TakeOver**, **Search Skills**, **Introductory Networking**, **Intro to LAN**, **DNS in Detail** and **HTTP in Detail**.

## Exploitation and post-exploitation

I gained first practical exposure to offensive tooling and exploitation workflows.

Tools used include:

- Nmap
- Dirb
- Hydra
- Metasploit / msfconsole

I practiced credential brute-forcing in authorized labs, exploitation with Metasploit, obtaining reverse shells on authorized Linux targets and basic Linux privilege escalation.

The goal during this phase was not to memorize isolated commands, but to understand how reconnaissance, exploitation and post-exploitation fit together.
