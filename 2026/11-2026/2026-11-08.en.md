## Diffie–Hellman key exchange

I also studied the mathematical relationships used in the Diffie–Hellman key exchange:

- (A = g^a \bmod p): Alice's public value.
- (B = g^b \bmod p): Bob's public value.
- (K = B^a \bmod p = A^b \bmod p): the shared secret independently computed by both parties.

This helped me understand how two parties can establish a shared secret without directly transmitting it.
