Today I completed the [**Wireshark: The Basics**](https://tryhackme.com/room/wiresharkthebasics) room, focused on analysing network captures with Wireshark.

## Packet dissection

I spent more time on packet dissection to understand how Wireshark breaks down network exchanges by layers and protocols. The goal was to go beyond the packet list and inspect the fields and information available in each packet: addresses, timestamps, protocol fields and transported data.

## Packet navigation

I practiced navigating a capture: locating specific packets, searching for strings and information in packet details, and following exchanges to understand the context of a communication. An individual packet is not always enough; it often needs to be understood as part of a broader conversation.

## Packet filtering

I also practiced display filters to reduce the number of visible packets and isolate relevant traffic. Filtering is essential when working with large captures, helping focus an investigation on a specific protocol or exchange.
