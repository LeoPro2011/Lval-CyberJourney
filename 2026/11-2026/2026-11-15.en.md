# 15 November 2026 — TryHackMe: Blue

I completed the **[Blue](https://tryhackme.com/room/blue)** room and earned the **Blue — Hacking into Windows via EternalBlue** badge.

This room provides hands-on practice exploiting a Windows machine vulnerable to **MS17-010 (EternalBlue)**, a vulnerability affecting SMBv1. The workflow connects network reconnaissance, vulnerability identification, exploitation with Metasploit, and working inside a Meterpreter session.

The main learning point was moving from initial access to a Meterpreter session for post-exploitation work in the lab. It helped me connect networking fundamentals and my first Metasploit exercises to a concrete Windows compromise scenario.

The room also covers privilege verification, process migration, hash extraction and cracking, and locating three flags. I describe these as room objectives here; I am not claiming that every individual step was independently completed and documented.

**Key concepts:**
- SMBv1 and exposure of the SMB service on TCP/445;
- MS17-010 / EternalBlue and remote code execution;
- Metasploit, sessions and Meterpreter;
- the difference between a standard shell and Meterpreter;
- post-exploitation and the importance of privilege level;
- the defensive value of applying MS17-010 patches and disabling SMBv1 where it is no longer required.

Separately, I reused my networking and IT knowledge to transfer mods to my console over FTP. This was a personal application of file-transfer and network-service concepts, distinct from the TryHackMe lab.

**Badge earned: Blue.** I want to revisit some Meterpreter and post-exploitation concepts to understand them more deeply, beyond simply completing the room.

All exploitation was performed in the designated training lab.

Sources: [TryHackMe — Blue](https://tryhackme.com/room/blue) · [Microsoft Security Bulletin MS17-010](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/security-updates/securitybulletins/2017/ms17-010).
