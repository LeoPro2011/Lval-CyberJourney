# 1 November 2026 — TryHackMe: Networking Secure Protocols

Today, I completed the [**Networking Secure Protocols**](https://tryhackme.com/room/networkingsecureprotocols) room, the final room in the Networking fundamentals sequence.

## Secure protocols

I studied how network communications and the protocols carrying data can be secured. In particular, I learned about **TLS (Transport Layer Security)** and how it provides confidentiality, integrity and authentication. I also connected familiar protocols with their secure variants, such as HTTP/HTTPS and secure email protocols.

## File transfer: SFTP and FTPS

Through a practical exercise, I explored the differences between **SFTP**, which runs over SSH, and **FTPS**, which adds TLS to FTP. I now have a clearer understanding that these are two distinct ways to secure file transfers, even though they serve similar purposes.

## VPN

I also gained a more concrete understanding of a **VPN (Virtual Private Network)**. It creates a secure communication tunnel across a network that cannot necessarily be trusted. I had heard of VPNs before, but did not really understand how they worked.

## First steps with Wireshark

The room also gave me some initial hands-on experience with **Wireshark** to inspect network traffic:
- navigating a capture and inspecting packets;
- applying display filters to isolate specific exchanges;
- examining protocol details and gradually understanding what is actually happening on the network.

Wireshark is quite difficult to use at first: reading packets and applying the right filters takes practice. However, as I worked through the exercises, I gradually began to understand what I was looking at. I will need more practice to become comfortable and independent with this tool.
