## 23 October 2026 — Active Directory: structure and authentication

I completed a TryHackMe room focused on **Active Directory**, continuing the exploration I started the previous day.

### Organisation and administration

I studied how an Active Directory environment is organised, including the structure of objects and the use of **Group Policy Objects (GPOs)** to apply settings to users and machines.

### Authentication: Kerberos and NetNTLM

I learned about **Kerberos** as an authentication protocol in Windows domain environments, as well as **NetNTLM**, an older challenge-response-based mechanism. They work differently and have different security implications, so it is important to understand their respective roles rather than conflate them.

### Domains, trees, forests and trusts

I then studied the relationships between domains, **trees**, **forests**, and **trusts**. These concepts explain how Active Directory structures multiple domains and how trust relationships can enable access between them.

### Key takeaways

- Active Directory organisation and object management
- Group Policy Objects (GPOs)
- Kerberos authentication
- NetNTLM and challenge-response authentication
- Domains, trees, forests, and trust relationships
