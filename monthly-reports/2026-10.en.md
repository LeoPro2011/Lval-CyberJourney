# October 2026 Review

October focused on consolidating core foundations, with significant progress in **networking**, **Windows/Linux systems**, and **investigation**. I also started connecting technical concepts to practical cybersecurity use cases.

## Technical progress

- **Networking and services:** OSI and TCP/IP models, IPv4 and subnetting, encapsulation, TCP/UDP, SMB, FTP, NFS, SSH, SMTP, and MySQL. Practised service enumeration and analysis with Nmap, Netcat, Metasploit, and Hydra.
- **Reconnaissance:** WHOIS, DNS, `nslookup`, `dig`, DNSDumpster, and Shodan; distinction between passive and active reconnaissance.
- **Wi-Fi:** introduction to 802.11 frames, monitor mode, access points, and stations.
- **Windows and Active Directory:** Windows administration tools, command line, PowerShell, GPOs, Kerberos, NetNTLM, domains, forests, and trust relationships.
- **Linux and command line:** command-line practice, filesystem navigation, and introductory system administration.
- **Application and memory security:** SQL injection in a lab, study of a PHP vulnerability involving `session.upload_progress`, and introduction to dangling pointers / use-after-free issues.
- **Investigation and defence:** AI Forensics room, `auth.log` analysis, artefact correlation, and first exposure to Splunk and threat hunting.

## Training and milestones

- **TryHackMe — Pre Security:** path completed at 100%.
- **Cisco Packet Tracer:** training completed and certificate earned.
- **Cisco Networking Academy — Introduction to Cybersecurity:** course certificate earned.
- **TryHackMe — Cybersecurity 101:** continued work on Windows, Linux, command-line, and networking foundations.
- **Cisco Junior Cybersecurity Analyst:** selected as a complementary learning path to follow gradually during the school year.

## Reflection and direction

Networking practice reinforced the importance of understanding services and how they interact before attempting to exploit them. AI Forensics and threat-hunting exercises also increased my interest in investigation, SOC, and DFIR, alongside offensive security.

My approach remains exploratory: strengthen networking and systems foundations, continue authorised labs, and progressively explore defensive security, cloud, and AI applied to cybersecurity.
